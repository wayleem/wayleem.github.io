Three things shipped together because they're the same feature seen from
different angles: knowing what you don't know.

The mastery dashboard reads per-unit, per-course and cross-course, sliced by
format, scope, and activity. Study modes put the existing applied/recall tagging
and difficulty to actual use instead of just storing it. And any graded question
can now open into a tutor conversation — you ask why the answer was wrong, in
context, without leaving the quiz.
